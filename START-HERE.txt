BEKLO v2.1 - START HERE
=======================

Do these in order. Fix the backend first, look at the app last.

The first time you open a .command file, your Mac may say it "cannot be
opened". If so: right-click the file > Open > Open. Only once per file.


STEP 1 - SEND THE NEW BACKEND ONLINE
   Double-click:  UPDATE BACKEND.command
   - Wait. The first time it can be quiet for a minute while it gets ready.
   - Vercel shows a LINK and a CODE. Press Enter if it asks, or copy the
     link into your browser. Sign in with the account that has voom-backend.
   - When it asks for the admin phone, type the phone you use in the app,
     like +2519XXXXXXXX.
   - Wait 1-2 minutes. When you see {"status":"ok"}, the backend is updated.


STEP 2 - CHECK THE BOSS PAGE WORKS
   Open https://voom-backend-kappa.vercel.app/admin
   Sign in with the EMAIL and PASSWORD of the account that uses your admin
   phone. New drivers show up here with their Fayda ID and photos.
   Drivers cannot take any rides until you press Approve.


STEP 3 - PUT THIS VERSION ON GITHUB
   See GITHUB-UPDATE.txt. This stops your versions splitting again.


STEP 4 - NOW LOOK AT THE APP ON YOUR PHONE
   Install "Expo Go" on your phone.
   Double-click:  OPEN IN EXPO GO.command
   Scan the QR code. The backend address is already inside.
   (Or: REVIEW BEKLO IN BROWSER.command for a quick look on the Mac.)


STEP 5 - LATER: THE REAL IPHONE APP
   Needs an Apple Developer account ($99/year) + a free Expo account.
   Double-click:  BUILD BEKLO IPHONE.command and follow the 4 steps.
